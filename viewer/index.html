    Terrain screening, not a flood prediction. This map knows the shape of the ground, and how much rain the land cover and soil above it shed — not the sewers and culverts beneath it. It is published for public understanding: not for insurance, real-estate, or emergency decisions, and it carries no warranty. Pool names are the nearest named feature, not the pool's identity.

    USGS 3DEP 1m lidar DEM (5 projects, 2016–2023; IL 4-County QL1 2016 over most of the region) · deep pools near the Des Plaines and Sag corridors are working limestone quarries, not flood hollows · west Will County has no 3DEP 1m coverage and is blank · water, names & address search © OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) · county boundaries US Census TIGER 2024 · city boundary Chicago Data Portal · basemap USGS The National Map · design storms ISWS Bulletins 70 & 75 (NE Illinois, 24-hr) · rain scenarios turn rain into runoff cell by cell with the NRCS curve number method, from NLCD 2021 land cover and imperviousness (MRLC) and SSURGO hydrologic soil groups (USDA) · the one uniform assumption left is 10 mm/event of sewer and inlet capture, which is an assumption, not data — the network is not public · v0.4 (Aug 2026): per-cell runoff replaced a uniform coefficient — the 1 in map shrank, the design-storm maps grew · scored twice against Chicago 311 flooding reports (honestly: modest skill for streets, none for basements) — method & numbers in docs/VALIDATION.md on GitHub.
