Bluespot

where the water wants to go — Chicago's North Side

Blue marks every place the bare earth would hold water if rain outran drainage, computed by filling a 1-meter USGS lidar terrain model (depression depth, ≥ 5 cm). Deeper blue, deeper pool — the deepest are the rail viaducts you already know flood.

Terrain screening, not a flood prediction. This map knows the shape of the ground — not the sewers, culverts, or soil beneath it. It shows where water would gather first, not whether it will.

Sources: USGS 3DEP 1m DEM (IL 4-County QL1 lidar) · water outlets © OpenStreetMap contributors · basemap Esri World Topographic
